Privacy

Holdfire stores your TypeSafe API key in Chrome sync storage on devices signed into your browser profile.

When a risky click is intercepted, page title, URL, a short text snippet, and the button label are sent to api.typesafe.ai so Jev can decide. We do not operate a Holdfire backend. We do not sell data. No analytics by default.

You can uninstall anytime. Clearing extension storage removes the key and stats.
