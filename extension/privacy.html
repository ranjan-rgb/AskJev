AskJev privacy

AskJev is a Chrome/Brave extension. We do not operate AskJev servers that receive your browsing data.

What we store

Settings live in Chrome sync storage on your Google account: TypeSafe API key, sensitivity, keywords, allowlist, optional agent-bridge pairing token, and local stats counters.

What leaves your browser

  • TypeSafe only: when Guard or Autopilot needs a decision, AskJev sends page/action context to https://api.typesafe.ai with your TypeSafe API key.
  • Agent bridge (optional): if you enable it, the extension talks to a local process on ws://127.0.0.1 only. That traffic does not go to AskJev servers.

What we do not do

No AskJev analytics backend. No sale of browsing data. No remote code from AskJev. Claude/Cursor only connect if you set up Auto-connect on your machine.
